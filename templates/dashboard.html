    Automatic appointments

    When a patient asks on WhatsApp to book, cancel or reschedule and the schedule checks all pass (slot free, inside clinic hours, not in the past, not in a booking block, their own appointment, within limits), the app does it straight away and tells them. Anything that fails a check goes to Patient messages for you, with the reason. Everything else (registration, fees, follow-ups, staff voice commands) still needs your approval.

    {{ 'Automatic appointments are ON' if automation.enabled else 'Automatic appointments are OFF -- every request waits for your approval' }}
    {{ automation.used_today }} of {{ automation.daily_cap }} used today

    The cap limits how many bookings the automation creates per calendar day (a safety valve against a flood of requests, not appointment capacity). Beyond it, new booking requests wait for you. Cancels and reschedules are not counted.

    Booking blocks (no new appointments)

    Block a day, several days, or a time range on those days -- the doctor is away, the clinic is being renovated. Patients are never offered blocked times, and nobody can be booked into them without a confirmed override. Appointments that already exist are not touched; they are listed so you can handle them.

    Leave both times empty to block whole days.

    No blocks.

    Close a branch (and move its patients)

    The doctor is ill, or a branch must shut for a few days. This stops new bookings in that window and proposes a new branch and time for every patient already booked, all in one batch. You review and change each row, then apply: the moves are saved, and each patient is told on WhatsApp and can Accept or Choose another. The whole closure can be undone.

    Leave both times empty to close whole days.
